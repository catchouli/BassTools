Every phrase comes from a rules-based algorithm running in your browser — a chord progression, a difficulty-tiered rhythm library, and voice-leading rules over the key you pick. It plays each phrase once, then loops silently over the same bars for your response before moving on to the next one, which fades in below while you play. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
